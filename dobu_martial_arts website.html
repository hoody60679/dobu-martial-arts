Our Story

About DoBu

Founded in 2009, DoBu Martial Arts has grown from a small community club into one of the Midlands' most respected martial arts training centres.

Who We Are

Built on Discipline,
Driven by Community

DoBu Martial Arts was founded by Damian Burke in 2009 with a simple mission: to provide accessible, world-class martial arts training to people of all backgrounds, ages and abilities in the Solihull area.

What started as a 20-person BJJ class in a rented sports hall has grown into a 500-member training community offering four disciplines, six qualified coaches, and a purpose-built 4,000 sq ft training facility.

We believe martial arts training builds more than just physical fitness. The discipline, resilience and community developed on the mat carry into every aspect of life.

What to Expect

Class Details

🥋

BJJ

Classes begin with a warm-up, followed by technique drilling and positional sparring. Gi and No-Gi sessions available. UKBJJA affiliated.

🥊

Muay Thai

Bag work, pad work, clinch training and sparring available depending on level. Safety gear provided for beginners. WBC MT affiliated.

⚡

Judo

British Judo Association affiliated. Grading pathway available. Randori (free practice) and Kata sessions throughout the week.

🔥

MMA

Combines striking and grappling. Beginners focus on fundamentals. Advanced sessions include live rounds with full protective gear.
